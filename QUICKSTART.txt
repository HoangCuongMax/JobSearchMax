================================================================================
                   JOB CRAWLER & DASHBOARD - QUICK START
================================================================================

You now have a complete job tracking system! Here's how to get started:

STEP 1: OPEN THE DASHBOARD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Open job_dashboard.html in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
• Bookmark it for quick access
• All data is saved locally in your browser


STEP 2: ADD YOUR FIRST JOBS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Option A - Copy sample data:
  1. Open sample_jobs.json
  2. Copy all the content
  3. In dashboard, click "Import Jobs"
  4. Paste and click "✅ Import Jobs"
  5. You'll see 5 example jobs loaded!

Option B - Add your own jobs:
  1. Click "Import Jobs" in dashboard
  2. Paste your own job listing as JSON
  3. Each job needs: title, company, location, salary, url, platform, description


STEP 3: SET YOUR KEYWORDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Type keywords separated by commas: "Python, Data, AWS"
• The dashboard will score each job 0-100% based on matches
• Green cards = high match (75%+)
• Yellow cards = medium match (50-75%)
• Red cards = low match (<50%)


STEP 4: FILTER & FIND OPPORTUNITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Location: Type "Sydney" to find jobs in that area
• Salary: Use sliders to set min/max salary range
• Platform: Filter by LinkedIn, Seek, Indeed, etc.
• Status: Filter by New, Applied, Interested, Rejected

Click "Reset Filters" to clear all at once


STEP 5: TRACK YOUR APPLICATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Each job card has buttons:
  • "Apply" - Mark as applied (button turns green)
  • "View" - Open job link in new tab
  • "Delete" - Remove from your list

Add notes to each job for reminders (upcoming interview, salary negotiation notes, etc.)


STEP 6: SET UP DAILY CRAWLING (OPTIONAL)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

For now, manual imports work great. But if you want automation:

ON WINDOWS:
  1. Open job_crawler.py
  2. Uncomment the example at the bottom to add jobs
  3. Open Task Scheduler
  4. Create a task to run "python job_crawler.py" daily at 9 AM
  
ON MAC/LINUX:
  1. Edit your crontab: crontab -e
  2. Add: 0 9 * * * /usr/bin/python3 /path/to/job_crawler.py
  3. Save and exit

Then jobs will update automatically every day!


STEP 7: CUSTOMIZE JOB SOURCES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Edit sources.json to add your job search URLs:

  {
    "custom_urls": [
      "https://careers.company.com/jobs",
      "https://seek.com.au/python-jobs",
      "https://indeed.com.au/jobs?q=data"
    ]
  }

COMMON QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Locally on your computer, in your browser storage. No cloud uploads.

Q: Can I use this on my phone?
A: Yes! Open job_dashboard.html on mobile Safari or Chrome.

Q: How do I backup my data?
A: In dashboard, right-click → "Inspect" → Console → 
   localStorage.getItem('jobs') → copy the output

Q: Can I share jobs with a friend?
A: Yes! Export your jobs JSON and they can import it into their dashboard.

Q: What if JavaScript is disabled?
A: The dashboard needs JavaScript. Enable it in your browser settings.
